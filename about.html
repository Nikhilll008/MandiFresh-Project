The Project

About MandiFresh

A frontend prototype for a real problem: farmers often sell without knowing whether today's price is fair, or what it means for their margin.

Mission

Give every farmer the same information a trader has

MandiFresh exists to close the information gap between mandi traders and the farmers who supply them. By surfacing daily rates, 30-day trends and a simple profit calculator in one place, a farmer can walk into negotiation — or plan a season — with real numbers instead of guesswork.

The platform tracks prices across 10 mandis and 50+ crops, and lets anyone estimate revenue, cost and break-even price before a single kg is sold.

Process

How It Works

Four steps from opening the dashboard to knowing your number.

STEP 01

Select Crop

Choose a crop from over 50 tracked commodities, from onion and tomato to turmeric and cotton.

STEP 02

View Mandi Prices

Compare current and previous rates across partner mandis to see where the price is strongest.

STEP 03

Analyze Trend

Read the 30-day chart to check if a price is climbing, falling, or holding steady before you sell.

STEP 04

Calculate Profit

Enter quantity, cost and selling price to get revenue, net profit or loss, and your break-even rate.
